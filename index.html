01The Ten-Session Arc

One year, ten sessions, one region.

A progressive arc from foundations to culmination, meeting on second Fridays, 10:00 – 11:30 AM PT. Completed sessions link to their full archive — recording, brief, demonstrations, and featured faculty work. Upcoming sessions show what’s ahead.

Series Progress 0 of 10 completed
02Four Pathways

Parallel entry points — not a ladder.

Each track serves a different faculty reality and exits with its own credential. The monthly convenings are where all four tracks meet in one room.

Track 01

Research & Professional Development

Foundational AI literacy for every faculty member — building the regional knowledge floor.

Exit · AI Literacy
Track 02

Curriculum Development

Formal Course Outline of Record revision to integrate AI into approved curriculum.

Exit · AI Curriculum Competency
Track 03

Instructional Practice Integration

Classroom AI experimentation in courses you’re teaching now — without formal COR revision.

Exit · Applied AI Fluency
Track 04

Train the Trainer

Designing the AI professional development frameworks that will scale this work across the region.

Exit · AI PD Architect
